Here is my phpMyAdmin report for March 2020.

<!-- more -->

In March 2020 I worked on performance: replacing the YAML dependency injection loader by a PHP array and adding a route parsing cache. I also did a lot of Debian uploads and translation cleanups on the side, and sent pull-requests to php-webdriver and db-migrate/mysql.

I upgraded the JS dependencies (codemirror, sass, eslint, stylelint) and added a script to sync the vendored JS files after upgrades. I improved the translation tooling: a match-twig-cache script, notes for the translators about parameter binding and source translations, and the update-po command on MacOSX. On localized_docs I fixed a lot of translation mistakes, the German files included. On sql-parser I fixed a TypeError on `trim()`.
