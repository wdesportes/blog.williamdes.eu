Report for May 2018

Author: William Desportes
Published: May 1, 2018 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 2 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for May 2018.

In May 2018 I worked on the main repository, on configuration and interface fixes.

First week (18)

I fixed the DefaultLang and Lang configuration (#14260) and the CodeMirror hints that were hidden (#14257). The Git commit info was not showing when the repository is a submodule; I fixed that (#14253) and added tests for the revision checks. I also landed the binary foreign key enhancement (#13894) and a patch for #14246.

  • e9d3fdd493 - Binary foreign key enhancement #13894
  • 8af0343bb2 - Patch for issue #14246

Second week (19)

I reverted my modifications that caused errors, then cherry-picked the DefaultLang and Lang fix to QA_4_8. On phpmyadmin/scripts I used Sami to generate the developer documentation (#13) and sent a small cleanup to Sami itself (#348).
