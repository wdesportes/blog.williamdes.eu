Report for July 2019

Author: William Desportes
Published: July 1, 2019 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 3 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for July 2019.

In July 2019 I worked on the error reporting server and the main repository, with the CakePHP 3.8 migration as the main piece of work.

First week (27)

My focus of the week was the error reporting server: I migrated it to CakePHP 3.8, added phpstan and applied the phpmyadmin coding standard with phpcs. The migration itself is mostly the work of Murod Khaydarov, who wrote it as a contribution (#181); I then fixed the deprecations it revealed, the routes, the timestamps, the microhistory and the unit tests. On the main repository I fixed undefined indexes and errors when reporting errors.
