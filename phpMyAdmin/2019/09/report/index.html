Report for September 2019

Author: William Desportes
Published: September 1, 2019 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 5 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for September 2019.

In September 2019 I worked on the main repository, docker, sql-parser and the Debian packaging. On the side I fixed the deprecated snake_case usage in pixelpeter/laravel5-isocodes-validation (#15).

First week (36)

I worked on the CI: I moved to xenial while keeping PHP 5.5 on a trusty custom step, used NodeJS 10 on the release step and fixed the OSX build, cherry-picking Maurício's work and removing sudo from the step. I also reverted the floating menubar fix, it was not the right fix. On the designer I fixed the page saving and reloading: the page number is cast to an int, -1 or positive, the URL and the page status are correct after saving (#15440, #15439) and the full screen icon says enter mode again. On docker I merged four pull-requests: documentation for using secrets, a cleanup of unused files with a configuration improvement, the generate-stackbrew-library.sh script and a makefile fix. I also opened a pull-request to rewrite the end to end tests with Cucumber and Puppeteer (#15462).
