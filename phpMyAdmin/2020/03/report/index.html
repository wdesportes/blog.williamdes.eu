Report for March 2020

Author: William Desportes
Published: March 1, 2020 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 17 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for March 2020.

In March 2020 I worked on performance: replacing the YAML dependency injection loader by a PHP array and adding a route parsing cache. I also did a lot of Debian uploads and translation cleanups on the side, and sent pull-requests to php-webdriver and db-migrate/mysql.

First week (9)

I did the Debian packaging of motranslator 5.0.0, with package tests, DEB_BUILD_OPTIONS support, debhelper 12, Standards-Version 4.5.0 and myself in the Uploaders field.
