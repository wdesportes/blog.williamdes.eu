Report for November 2020

Author: William Desportes
Published: November 1, 2020 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 12 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for November 2020.

In November 2020 I fixed bugs, worked on PHP 8 compatibility and moved the API documentation build to Doctum in all our repositories.

First week (44)

I merged a few fixes: the SameSite=Strict cookie support, the Windows error message using a trailing / instead of a \, and a BINARY comparison when looking at privileges. I also updated phpstan and the po files.
