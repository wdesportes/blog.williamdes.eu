Author: William Desportes
Published: February 1, 2022 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 6 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for February 2022.

In February 2022 my focus was bug fixing and the Debian package of 5.1.3.

First week (5)

I fixed the ODS import issues #17298 and #17344, and the "First day of calendar" setting that had no effect. I also fixed the PHP type error in Results.php (#17315) and the MariaDB case about disabled_storage_engines (#17331).
