Author: William Desportes
Published: March 1, 2022 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 4 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for March 2022.

In March 2022 my focus was tests and CI, plus a few fixes.

First week (9)

I fixed #16746 by removing the use of the deprecated strftime in OpenDocument exports, and wrote a test for the OpenDocument class. I merged #17191 to fix the GIS visualization with an edited query (#17188), and #17377 for an incorrect usage of escapeMysqlWildcards.
