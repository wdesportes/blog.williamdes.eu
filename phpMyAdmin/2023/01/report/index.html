Report for January 2023

Author: William Desportes
Published: January 1, 2023 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 12 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for January 2023.

In January 2023 my focus was the sql-parser 5.6.0 and 5.7.0 releases and the Debian packages.

First week (1)

I merged #18007 to fix the UUID columns that could not be moved, and updated the sql-parser dependency to 5.6.0. On sql-parser I released 5.6.0 after merging #385 for the lexer ambiguity on function keywords and #397 for a performance improvement on nextToken().
