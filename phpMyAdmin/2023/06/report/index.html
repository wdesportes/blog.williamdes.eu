Author: William Desportes
Published: June 1, 2023 12:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 6 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for June 2023.

In June 2023 my focus was the sql-parser 5.8.0 release and the docker image.

First week (22)

I merged #18474 to require at least PHP 8.1.2 on phpmyadmin, and #407 to add a no-network option on the Alpine Dockerfile. On sql-parser I merged a series of code cleanups (#454 to #459), including the removal of the Misc class and the native return types.
