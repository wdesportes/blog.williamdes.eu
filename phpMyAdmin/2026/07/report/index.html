Report for July 2026

Author: William Desportes
Published: October 7, 2026 18:00 and updated: November 10, 2026 12:00 · 1 min read

Here is my phpMyAdmin report for July 2026.

In July I merged and reviewed contributions, did some issues triage and kept up the Debian packaging of our dependencies.

First week (28)

I merged contributions after reviewing them. I also did some issues triage. I fixed compressed columns not being detected on MariaDB, a TypeError when adding a column without index data, and some test issues with PHPUnit 13.1. I updated the psalm baseline and worked on the release tooling (create-release.sh, and the list of files excluded from a release tarball).

I also made twig-i18n-extension compatible with Twig 3.26+, and migrated the motranslator Debian watch file to version 5.

Second week (29)

I did some issues triage.

Third week (30)

Nothing to report

Fourth week (31)

I sent twig-i18n-extension 5.0.2-1 to unstable, bumped the Standards-Version and dropped the patches that are now upstream.
